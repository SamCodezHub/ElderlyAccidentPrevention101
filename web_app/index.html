Frequently Asked Questions

How does fall detection work without cameras?
The Hertz and Haven Company uses 24GHz FMCW mmWave radar — the same technology in self-driving cars. It sends invisible radio waves that bounce off objects and people. The AI analyzes the reflected signals to detect sudden changes in movement patterns that indicate a fall. It works through walls, in total darkness, and doesn't capture any visual images — preserving complete privacy.
Does my parent need to wear anything?
No. The radar sensors are mounted on walls and are completely invisible. Your parent doesn't need to wear any device, press any button, or change any habit. The system works passively in the background — true set-and-forget protection.
Is my family's data safe?
All data — including passwords, session tokens, health information, and floor plans — is stored securely in your browser's local storage. Passwords are salted and hashed before storage, and session tokens are rotated on sign-in. Nothing is sent to external servers. You have complete ownership and control of your data.
What happens when a fall is detected?
The system triggers a multi-step emergency response: (1) Family caregivers receive push notifications immediately, (2) SMS and automated voice calls ensure reachability, (3) You can confirm or dismiss the alert from the dashboard, (4) If confirmed, healthcare teams are deployed with live GPS tracking to the patient's location.
How is the floor plan analysis done?
Upload a photo, sketch, or digital floor plan of your parent's home. Our AI spatial analysis engine scans the layout, identifies high-risk fall zones (bathrooms, staircases, kitchen), and recommends optimal positions for up to 12 radar sensors. The analysis takes under 10 seconds and includes confidence scores for each placement.
What does "Family Gateway" mean?
Family Gateway is the caregiver-facing dashboard of The Hertz and Haven Company. It's your single command centre — view sensor status, monitor room activity, manage emergency contacts, control smart lock OTPs, track deployed healthcare teams, and receive alerts. It works on any browser, from anywhere in the world.
